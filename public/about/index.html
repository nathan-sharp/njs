About

Nathan James Sharp is a Mechanical Engineering Apprentice and software tool builder based in Aberdeen, Scotland.

Who I Am

I am currently a Mechanical Engineering Apprentice at an energy company in Aberdeen, working primarily on mechanical design, 3D CAD modeling with Autodesk Inventor, and producing precision technical engineering drawings using AutoCAD. I hold an NC Level 6 in Mechanical Engineering from North East Scotland College (NESCol) and am actively completing my SVQ Level 3 qualification.

In addition to mechanical and manufacturing engineering, I build practical software tools. My day-to-day design and engineering work involves frequent calculations and reference lookups: tasks where existing online utilities are often cluttered with ads, require sign-ups, or hide the underlying mathematics. NJS.dev was born out of solving those practical problems directly: building fast, transparent, and completely client-side tools for engineers, technicians, and students.

What NJS.dev Is

NJS.dev is my personal site where I host free, browser-based engineering tools, technical calculators, and reference guides. Every utility on this site runs entirely client-side inside your browser: there are no backend servers processing your inputs, and zero bytes of your data leave your device. You never need to create an account or log in to use anything here.

The tools cover daily engineering challenges and technical domains I work in or find interesting: precise angle conversions, thread dimension lookups across global standards (Unified Inch, ISO Metric, NPT, and BSPT), gauge block calculations for sine bars, unit conversions that explicitly show the working formulas, local LabVIEW TDMS telemetry file analysis, and interactive physics simulations.

The design philosophy is strictly minimal: no pop-ups, no tracking barriers beyond legal necessities, and no dark patterns. The goal is to provide engineering tools that are fast, dependable, and genuinely pleasant to use.
