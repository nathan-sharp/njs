Notation

This is a draft symbol list for a more universal notation system. The goal is to reduce overlap by assigning one primary meaning to each symbol.

It is intentionally simple and uses plain HTML only.

General Science Coverage

  • Math: variables, operators, relations, calculus-style change, and logic.
  • Physics: mechanics, electromagnetism, thermodynamics, and measurement units.
  • Chemistry: amount of substance, molar mass, charge, oxidation state, concentration, and temperature.
  • Biology: statistics, concentration, and general quantitative modeling terms.
  • Engineering: force, pressure, resistance, energy, work, volume, and units.
  • Statistics: mean, spread, uncertainty, probability, and comparison symbols already listed above.

The list is still not a complete replacement for field-specific notation, but it now covers a much wider share of general science use.
