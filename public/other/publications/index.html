Typesetting Architecture: Typst vs. LaTeX

While traditional academic publishing relies heavily on legacy LaTeX distributions (often multi-gigabyte installations with slow single-threaded macro expansion), our research publications are formatted using **Typst**—a modern, Rust-engineered typesetting system:

  • Incremental Compilation & Performance: Typst utilizes a deterministic layout engine written in Rust that achieves sub-millisecond compilation times through incremental AST evaluation and memoized layout passes.
  • Declarative Styling (`conf.typ`): Instead of complex TeX macro hacking, layout constraints, theorem boxes, and bibliography formatting (`template.bib`) are defined cleanly via functional programming primitives and scoped styling rules.
