Format Specifications: STL vs. STEP

Computer-Aided Design (CAD) and additive manufacturing rely on distinct interchange standards for 3D representation:

Specification Metric STL (Stereolithography) STEP (ISO 10303-21)
Geometry Type Tessellated triangular mesh Boundary Representation (B-Rep) & NURBS
Curved Surface Precision Approximated by discrete planar facets Exact mathematical analytical surfaces
Color & Body Metadata Typically monochrome mesh only Supports assembly hierarchy, body colors, and layers
Primary Industry Use 3D printing, CAM toolpaths, rapid prototyping CAD interchange, CNC machining, mechanical engineering
