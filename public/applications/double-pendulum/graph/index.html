Understanding the Phase Grid Matrix

This grid simultaneously integrates an array ($N \times N$) of independent double pendulums initialized across configuration space where the $X$ and $Y$ coordinates correspond to starting angles $\theta_1$ and $\theta_2$.

Heatmap RGB Color Mapping: Each cell's color encodes real-time angular positions ($R \propto \cos\theta_1, G \propto \cos\theta_2, B \propto \cos(\theta_1+\theta_2)$). In Pixel Mode, this reveals intricate fractal boundaries and bifurcations where tiny shifts in initial angle produce completely different color states over time.

Interactive Navigation: Click and drag inside the simulation window to pan around the grid. Scroll or use your mouse wheel over the window to zoom smoothly in and out on complex phase boundaries.
