LO 1: Recognizing Numbers

Numbers are everywhere! We use them to count things, tell the time, and know how old we are. In this lesson, we will look at numbers from 1 to 10.

Counting to 10

Let's count from 1 to 10 together:

  • 1 (One) - like one apple.
  • 2 (Two) - like two shoes.
  • 3 (Three) - like three sides of a triangle.
  • 4 (Four) - like four legs on a dog.
  • 5 (Five) - like five fingers on a hand.
  • 6 (Six) - like six sides on a honeycomb.
  • 7 (Seven) - like seven colors in a rainbow.
  • 8 (Eight) - like eight legs on a spider.
  • 9 (Nine) - like nine planets (including Pluto!).
  • 10 (Ten) - like ten toes on your feet.

Finding Numbers

Can you find numbers around you? Look at a clock, a phone, or a book. Practice pointing at a number and saying its name!
