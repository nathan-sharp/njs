LO 2: Basic Shapes

Shapes are all around us! Everything you see has a shape. Let's learn about some common shapes we see every day.

Circles

A circle is perfectly round. It has no straight lines and no corners.

Examples of Circles:

  • A coin.
  • A full pizza.
  • The wheels on a car.

Squares

A square has four straight sides that are all the same length. It also has four corners.

Examples of Squares:

  • A slice of sandwich bread.
  • A checkerboard.
  • The keys on a computer keyboard.

Triangles

A triangle has three straight sides and three corners.

Examples of Triangles:

  • A slice of pizza.
  • The sails on a boat.
  • A slice of pie.
