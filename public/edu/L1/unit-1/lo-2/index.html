LO 2: Basic Addition

Now that we know our numbers, let's learn how to add them together! Addition means putting things together to see how many there are in total.

Adding One

Adding one to a number is like counting up by one. If you have 1 apple and you get 1 more, you now have 2 apples! Let's look at some examples:

  • 1 + 1 = 2
  • 2 + 1 = 3
  • 3 + 1 = 4
  • 4 + 1 = 5

Adding Small Numbers

You can also add numbers like 2 and 3. You can use your fingers to help you count!

  • 2 + 2 = 4 (Two fingers on one hand, two fingers on the other!)
  • 3 + 2 = 5
  • 4 + 2 = 6
