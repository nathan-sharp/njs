LO 3: Basic Subtraction

We have learned how to add numbers, now let's learn how to subtract them! Subtraction means taking things away to see how many are left.

Taking One Away

Taking one away from a number is like counting down by one. If you have 2 apples and you eat 1, you now have 1 apple left! Let's look at some examples:

  • 2 - 1 = 1
  • 3 - 1 = 2
  • 4 - 1 = 3
  • 5 - 1 = 4

Taking More Away

You can also take away more than one. You can use your fingers or toys to help you understand!

  • 4 - 2 = 2 (Put up 4 fingers, then put down 2!)
  • 5 - 3 = 2
  • 6 - 4 = 2
