Educational Resources

A structured approach to engineering and technical subjects based on the Scottish Credit and Qualifications Framework (SCQF).

How to Use This Section

This section is organized hierarchically to mirror the Scottish qualification system:

  • Levels: The main categories correspond to SCQF levels. Each level represents a specific stage of learning progression.
  • Units: Inside each level, you will find subjects organized into specific SQA Units.
  • Learning Outcomes: Each unit is further broken down into specific Learning Outcomes, which contain the actual written guides, reference materials, and examples.

Select a level below to explore the available units and begin.

SCQF Levels

Level 4
National 4 equivalents. Foundational concepts and basic engineering principles.
Level 5
National 5 equivalents. Intermediate technical knowledge and applied skills.
Level 6
Higher equivalents. Advanced theoretical understanding and complex problem solving.
