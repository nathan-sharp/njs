Thermofluids

This unit introduces the fundamental principles of thermodynamics and fluid mechanics. It is essential for understanding energy transfer, power generation, and the behavior of fluids in engineering systems.

Unit Overview: By the end of this unit, you will be able to analyze fluid properties, apply the laws of thermodynamics, and evaluate the performance of basic thermofluid systems.
