Welcome to Level 6

Level 6 represents a significant step up in academic rigor and technical depth, equivalent to a Scottish Higher qualification. At this level, you are expected to handle complex, abstract concepts and apply them to solve multi-stage engineering problems.

Expectations: You should be comfortable with algebra, introductory calculus, and basic physical principles before beginning these units. The resources here focus heavily on theoretical analysis and detailed calculations that form the bedrock of mechanical engineering design.
