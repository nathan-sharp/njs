Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nathan James Sharp ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our applications and services, including apps published on Google Play and the Apple App Store.

Information We Collect

We design our apps to collect as little personal information as possible.

  • Information you provide directly: If an app allows contact forms or support requests, we collect the information you submit (such as your email address and message content).
  • Technical information: App stores and platform providers (Google, Apple) may provide us with aggregate, non-identifying diagnostics and download statistics.
  • On-device processing: Many app features process data locally on your device. We do not upload that content unless clearly stated inside the app.
  • Advertising data (for apps with ads): Apps that show Google Ads (including AdMob) may collect advertising identifiers, approximate location, device information, IP address, and app interaction data to serve ads, limit fraud, and measure ad performance.

How We Use Information

  • To provide and maintain app functionality.
  • To respond to support requests and user feedback.
  • To improve app reliability, performance, and usability.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share limited information only in the following cases:

  • With service providers that help us operate support or distribution services.
  • With advertising partners (such as Google) when an app includes ads.
  • When required by law, legal process, or to protect rights and safety.
  • As part of a business transfer (such as a merger or acquisition), where permitted by law.

Advertising

Some of our apps display ads provided by Google (for example, AdMob). Google and its partners may use cookies, SDKs, and similar technologies to provide and measure ads.

  • Ads may be personalized based on your advertising identifier and activity, where permitted by your device, region, and consent choices.
  • In some cases, apps may show non-personalized ads, which are based on contextual information rather than your profile.
  • You can manage ad personalization in your device privacy settings (including resetting or limiting use of your advertising identifier).

Third-Party Services

Our apps may be distributed through third-party platforms such as Google Play and the Apple App Store. Those platforms may collect information under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Advertising Technologies
  • Apple Privacy Policy

Children's Privacy

Our apps are not directed to children under 13 unless explicitly stated in the app listing. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

For apps intended for mixed or general audiences, ad settings and ad serving behavior may be adjusted to comply with applicable child privacy rules and store requirements.

Data Retention

We retain information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact Us
