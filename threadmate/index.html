ThreadMate

ThreadMate is a free engineering calculator for determining precision thread dimensions. Instantly calculate Pitch Diameter, Major Diameter, and manufacturing tolerances for Unified Inch (UN/UNR), ISO Metric, NPT, and ISO 7-1 (BSPT) threads. Simply select your standard and nominal size to generate ASME and ISO compliant data for machining and inspection.
